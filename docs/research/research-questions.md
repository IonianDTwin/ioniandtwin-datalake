# Research Questions

`PROPOSAL` Candidate research questions for the IonianDTwin university project. Not yet finalised or supervisor-approved.

## Digital Twin / sustainability

- RQ-1: How can a Digital Twin support sustainable tourism governance in the Ionian Islands at both NUTS 2 and municipal scale?
- RQ-2: Which composite indicators (tourist pressure, environmental burden, infrastructure resilience) best explain carrying-capacity stress in the region?

## Data and verification

- RQ-3: Which candidate data sources are publicly accessible, license-compliant, and technically reliable for the first vertical slice?
- RQ-4: How should participatory reports be cross-verified against sensor data to control for noise and fabrication?

## LLM / decision support

- RQ-5: How can a local-first LLM convert sustainability indicators into transparent, evidence-grounded policy suggestions without fabricating data?

## GSTC

- RQ-6: How well does the Data01–Data34 catalogue map to GSTC v2.0 criteria, and which gaps remain?

`RISK` These are unconfirmed. `DECISION` IonianDTwin has independent aims; research questions from other projects are excluded.
